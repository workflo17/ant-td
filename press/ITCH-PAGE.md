## Description (paste as the page body)

The pest bugs are marching on the colony's sugar stash. The ants answer.

**Grubs TD** is a hand-drawn tower defense about defending a picnic. Place worker
ants, trap-jaws, acid archers and honeypots along the trail; pop every bug before
it reaches the basket; survive all 40 rounds — then keep going into the storm.

Every pixel is drawn by code, live: the meadow on the title screen, the six maps
with their six hours of light, the ants themselves. No sprite sheets, no engine —
one canvas and a synthesized soundtrack that changes with every map and swells
when the swarm gets thick.

### What's inside

- **9 ant towers**, each with two upgrade paths and a once-per-game Ascension
- **6 backyard maps** — from the gentle Picnic Blanket to Night Porch after dark
  and the soap-slick Bath Time — each with its own light, music and gimmick
- **4 hero ants** with leveling and two active abilities each
- **40-round campaigns**, then freeplay overtime with storm ranks at r50/60/70
- **Foraging Run** — a roguelite mode: start with 3 random ants, draft unlocks
  and relics after every round
- **Daily Run** — one seeded loadout for everyone, plus a local rival colony
  that holds a grudge, escalates, and eventually respects you
- **Challenges, relics, colony perks, 20 achievements, star rewards** — and a
  guided first game that teaches by doing
- **Free, offline-capable** (installable PWA), phone-friendly, no accounts, no ads

### Controls

Mouse or touch for everything. Keyboard shortcuts: **1–9** pick an ant · **Z/X**
upgrades · **Space** start round · **F** speed · **Q/E/D** colony powers · **A/S**
hero abilities · **P** pause · **M** mute.
