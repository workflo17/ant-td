# The Owner Hour — hardware playtest checklist

The two things no headless bot can judge: **your phone's hands** and **your ears at 1×**.
One hour, one coffee. Note answers inline and file anything broken as a ROADMAP item.

## Setup (5 min)
- [ ] Phone, installed as PWA from https://workflo17.github.io/ant-td/ (fresh install,
      not a stale cached copy — check the build tag bottom corner).
- [ ] Desktop tab for the audio pass, volume at a normal evening level.

## Ears — desktop, 1× speed, rounds 1–24 on Hard (20 min)
- [ ] Round 1 pops: chunky or clicky? (Foley bank should thump, not tick.)
- [ ] Swarm round (r15+): does the compressor hold it together, or does it mush?
- [ ] Per-map themes: flip Picnic → Kitchen → Night Porch. Do they *feel* like
      different places? Does the ambient bed (birds/fridge hum/crickets) read?
- [ ] r24 stag: horn stinger + 1.5s dig-in — did your pulse move? If the charge
      feels unfair at 3× speed, say so (the telegraph compresses with game speed).
- [ ] Boss kill: hitstop + deep pop — satisfying or mushy?
- [ ] Settings sliders: set SFX 30% / music 70% — comfortable mix?

## Hands — phone, portrait then landscape (25 min)
- [ ] Place 5 ants in a hurry mid-round. Mis-taps? Ghost placement readable
      under a finger?
- [ ] Open a tower panel, buy 2 upgrades, change targeting mode, sell. All 44px+?
- [ ] Cast Acid Rain and a Decoy by hotkey-less tap — findable? Cancelable?
- [ ] Swarm round at 3×: frame feel — smooth, or soup? (If soup: note round + map;
      the sprite-atlas insurance in ROADMAP M6 only gets built if this happens.)
- [ ] NIGHT FALLS round (r51, or Nocturne relic): readable on a dim phone screen?
- [ ] Portrait: does the shop strip cover the exit lane? Landscape: HUD wrap sane?
- [ ] Battery/heat after 20 minutes: warm or worrying?

## Eyes — either device (10 min)
- [ ] Grayscale test (phone accessibility shortcut): can you still tell camo
      (stripes + dashed outline), burn (flames), slow (web lines), stun (stars)
      apart? All four have shape cues by design — confirm they carry.
- [ ] Reduced-flash toggle ON during a boss fight: any strobe left anywhere?
- [ ] The camo-warning chip, boss leak label, and 2ⁿᵈ-pot note: did you notice
      them without being told they exist?
